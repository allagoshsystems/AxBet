Types of Football Bets at AXBET

AXBET has an extensive range of wagers that players can choose from for football betting. These include single bets and accumulators. You can also find prop wagers such as yellow cards, corners, and more. 

Here are the different wagers found on the platform:

  1. Single bet: In this wager type, you bet on football matches, predicting a single outcome of a match or on a single event. The winnings are your stake amount multiplied by the odds.

  2. Chain Bet (where available): This is a wager that comprises multiple single bets. The stake of each bet is from the winnings of the previous wager in an order that the bettor specifies.

  3. Accumulator: When you add two or more selections to a single wager, you get the accumulator. Your selection should be accurate to win the wager, and the winnings are calculated by multiplying the odds of the selection.

  4. System wager: Similar-sized accumulators are wagered on a specific number of outcomes. You can win even if one of your selections is incorrect. This might be a safer wager than the standard accumulator.

  5. Anti-accumulator: In this type, you win if at least one of your selections is not correct. This wager is therefore the exact opposite of the accumulator wager.

  6. Multibet: This wager is a combination of single bets and accumulators. It might include the lobby bet, where the entire bet is reliant on the outcome of the lobby bet.

  7. Lucky Bet: This includes singles bets and accumulators with selections of a specific number. You need to get at least one of the selections right for a payout.

  8. Patent: This is a multiple bet of seven single bets across three match selections. You should select at least one correct selection to win.

When it comes to betting in football, the markets are as varied as the bet types at the site. Here are the popular markets and their description.

Match Winner

Double Chance

Football Betting Tips and Strategies

For newbie bettors and seasoned punters, making informed decisions is easier when the right approach is used. Here are some advanced strategies and tips that may guide you on improving your betting decisions:

  1. Research well: Before you start placing your bets, you need to look into aspects like the performance of an individual player or the team. Analyze the statistics like head-to-head clashes, recent wins, and more to help you make better decisions.

  2. Consider multiple markets: Try varied markets such as over/under, player-specific wagers, and more. The variety will help you understand the different markets and improve your betting.

  3. Try live betting: Betting on the game while it is underway provides you with the advantage of changing your bets during the game. You can observe the match dynamics before you place a wager. You might spot favorable odds as the game progresses.

  4. Bankroll management: Set limits on the amount of money you want to spend for each wager. Avoid persistent wagering. Bet using only a small percentage of your bankroll to start with. This will help to safeguard your funds and make them last longer.

  5. Gamble responsibly: Ensure you gamble responsibly. For instance, you can use the cash-out feature to settle a wager before a match ends. This allows you to potentially make a profit even if the match result is not as you predicted.

  6. Master value betting: Look for wagers that have better odds and payouts. You can find such wagers by understanding probabilities, like match circumstances, previous encounters, and player form. Wager on underdogs as they can offer rewarding odds.

  7. Monitor your wagers: Keep track of every wager you place, the odds, and the predicted outcome. With the logs providing all pertinent information of your approaches, you can evaluate their effectiveness and devise an improvement.
