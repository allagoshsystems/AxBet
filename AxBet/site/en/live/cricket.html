Live Cricket Betting Markets and In-Play Options

This page was created to help you understand everything in-play cricket betting. Since you already know how to place a live cricket bet, the next step is to understand the different in-play betting markets you see when you open the live section on our platform. 

  • Over-by-Over Markets: These markets focus on the outcome of a specific over. Examples include Total Runs, where you bet on whether the runs scored in a particular over will be over or under a set line (e.g., 5.5 runs). You could also bet on Wicket in Over to predict whether a wicket will fall during that specific over. 

  • Next-Over Props: Markets on specific happenings in the upcoming over that aren’t directly tied to the final match results. 

  • Wickets: Markets associated with a batter’s dismissal, such as Next Wicket Method (how the next batter will get out) and Runs at Fall of Wicket (total score of a team when the next wicket falls). You can also bet on the total number of wickets a team will lose in an innings.

  • Session Betting: Betting on what happens during a specific block of play. For instance, total runs or wickets lost during that session. 

  • Dynamic Totals: Also known as under or over betting. Offer over/under lines on the total innings score, with live odds that update continuously. 

  • Winner Markets: Predicting the eventual match winner (winning team) while the match is still ongoing. 

Unlike pre-match betting, the odds for live markets at the AXBET cricket section update continuously to reflect what’s happening in the game. You can access these live betting markets through our dedicated cricket live page. Alternatively, you can browse our entire sports lineup and switch to in-play betting mode to keep only the ongoing events.
